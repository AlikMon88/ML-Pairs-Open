# Key Types of Crypto "Fundamental" (On-Chain) Data

---

## Network Activity & Adoption

- **Active Addresses**  
  The number of unique addresses sending or receiving transactions.  
  A proxy for active users.

- **Transaction Count**  
  The total number of transactions on the network.  
  Indicates usage and adoption.

- **Transaction Volume**  
  The total value (e.g., in USD) being moved on the network.  
  Reflects economic throughput and activity.

---

## Network Security & Health

- **Hash Rate (for Proof-of-Work)**  
  The total computational power securing the network.  
  A higher hash rate means greater security and cost to attack.

- **Total Value Locked (TVL) (for DeFi)**  
  The amount of capital deposited into a protocol's smart contracts.  
  A key metric for the growth and trust in the DeFi ecosystem.

---

## Tokenomics & Valuation

- **Market Cap**  
  Formula: Price × Circulating Supply  
  Measures the total value of a crypto asset.

- **Circulating Supply vs. Total Supply**  
  Indicates how many coins are currently in circulation versus the maximum possible.  
  Helps assess inflation and scarcity.

- **"HODL Waves" / Age of Coins**  
  Shows the percentage of coins that haven’t moved in a long time.  
  Signals long-term conviction from holders and potential supply pressure.
